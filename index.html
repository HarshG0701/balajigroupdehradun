About Balaji Group

A local firm that walks the land it sells

Balaji Group works out of 10/17 Convent Road, above Axis Bank in Dehradun. The firm was established in 2023, and between them the partners bring more than a decade of work across property, investment and chartered accountancy.

  • Distances measured on site by GPS, never taken from a map

  • In-house chartered accountant for registration, taxation and structuring
